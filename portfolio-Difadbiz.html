Digital Signage System (Pre-HFI)

A tool to host content for and manage remote digital signages.

Background:

Digital Signage is a communication method which uses electronic medium instead of the traditional signages such as paper, plastic or metal billboards and displays. In the recent years, thanks to dropping prices and accessibility to quality screens, internet, and infrastructural advancements, the traditional methods demand more than a facelift.

Client:
DIF AdBiz
Services:

Strategy, UX Research, UX Design.

Designers:
Abhijeet Bhamare, Ganesh Gaikhe.
Time:
4 Months.

Design Process

This project involved two designers including me and people who handled work for marketing research, signage industry, and engineering. The design thought process involved decisions that were influenced by shared on-field experiences with these domain experts.

During the development process, the design went through lean feedback loop (build, measure, learn) making design improvements and increasing the usability of prototypes.

Peripheral Research

The static ad signages are becoming less significant by each passing day. People are being influenced, attracted, and catered to by rich engaging experiences via digital media.

This project initially started off only to digitalize static media but then drifted to be an indoor-outdoor digital signage solution that would enable anyone from anywhere to connect & use the system.


After conducting surveys and user interviews, we learned that there’s a great need for a solution that would promote businesses to different locations and reach end users there.

A product that would touch all business expert categories such as advertisement, sport, healthcare, hospitality, retail, agriculture, education, finance, government, construction, etc.

My survey results concluded that Advertising and business promotion ranked highest in terms of greatest digital video wall solution need by users.
